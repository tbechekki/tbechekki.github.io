Taha Echekki Designs is a small web design and development firm based in Charlotte, North Carolina, run by Taha Echekki. We build clean, fast, standards-compliant websites for small businesses, clubs, and individuals who want a site that looks good, works everywhere, and is easy to keep up to date.

We can take your project from a blank page to a finished site: planning the layout, choosing colors and type that fit your brand, writing semantic and accessible HTML and CSS, adding interactive features with JavaScript, and making sure everything validates and looks right on phones, tablets, and desktops.

  • Mobile-friendly layouts that adapt to any screen size.
  • Accessibility built in from the start, not added at the end.

  • Clear, honest pricing with no surprise fees.
  • Plain-language updates at every stage of the project.
  • Friendly support after launch for small changes and fixes.
